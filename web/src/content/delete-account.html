Delete your account

ClearSkin AI, by Bri Technologies

You can delete your ClearSkin AI account and all its data at any time, from the app or by email. You don’t need to cancel anything else first, but a Premium subscription is billed by Google Play, so cancel it there too: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

In the app

  1. Open ClearSkin AI and tap your initial at the top right to open Settings.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account & all data.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted straight away.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email britech.apps@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my ClearSkin account”. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, and your name and profile picture link if you used Google sign-in.
  • Your free-scan credits, invite code and invite record.
  • Everything stored on your phone: photos, scans, routine, check-ins, products and coach chats. (Uninstalling the app also deletes these.)

What is kept, and for how long

  • Usage counters (feature used, time, AI usage size and cost) are unlinked from you and expire within 90 days. They are kept that long only to stop free scans being reset by deleting and re-creating accounts.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat keep records of purchases as the law requires for payments and tax.

Photos sent for analysis are never stored on our servers, so there are none to delete there. More detail is in the privacy policy.
