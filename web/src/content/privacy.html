Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

The ClearSkin AI mobile app is made by Bri Technologies (Born Mugisha Brian, Tanzania), “we” in this policy. It explains what we process, why, and the choices you have.

In short

  • Your face photos, scan results, check-ins, products and coach chats are stored on your phone.
  • To analyse a scan, up to three photos (front and both sides) and the relevant skin details are sent, encrypted, to our AI provider. They are never stored on our servers, sold, or shared with advertisers. Side photos are not kept on your phone either.
  • We never use your photos to train AI.
  • The free version shows ads. Ads never receive your photos, skin data or answers. Premium has no ads.
  • You can export or permanently delete your data in the app at any time.

Advertising

Free users see a banner on the routine screen, an occasional full-screen ad after finishing a routine (limited per day, and never in your first days), and optional videos you can choose to watch for an extra scan.

  • Where the law requires consent, for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states, Google’s consent form asks you before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • For users aged 13 to 17 we request only non-personalised ads rated for teens or younger.
  • We block sensitive ad categories, including gambling, dating, alcohol and weight loss.
  • Premium removes all ads; the app makes no ad requests for Premium users.

Shopping links

Some routine steps may link to a search for that type of product at an online store in your region, based on your phone’s region setting. These can be affiliate links: we may earn a commission if you buy, at no cost to you. The store sees that you came from ClearSkin; we never send it your skin data. Our recommendations come from our routine engine and never depend on commissions.

Google account data

If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture link with ClearSkin. We use them only to create and secure your ClearSkin account. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers below that run the app. Deleting your account deletes it.

ClearSkin’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Service providers

  • Anthropic: AI analysis of scan photos and coach questions. Anthropic does not train its models on this data and keeps it only for a limited time for safety monitoring, under its commercial terms.
  • Supabase (Ireland): accounts, usage counters and rewarded-video credits.
  • Google: Google sign-in, sign-in code emails, and Google Play Integrity checks.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat: payments and subscription status.
  • Google AdMob, with its partners AppLovin, Meta Audience Network and Unity Ads: ads for free users.
  • Open-Meteo: weather for the city you choose.
  • Open Beauty Facts: product lookups (the barcode only).
  • PostHog (EU): anonymous analytics and crash reports.

Sensitive information

Skin photos and health-related details, such as pregnancy status or skin conditions, can count as sensitive information in some countries. We process them only with your explicit consent, given in the app before your first scan, and only to provide the features you use. We do not create face-recognition templates, identify you from photos, or share this data with advertisers.

Children

ClearSkin is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your choices and rights

  • Settings → Export my data gives you a copy of everything the app holds.
  • Settings → Delete account & all data deletes the data on your phone, your account, credits and purchase record. Usage counters are unlinked from you and expire within 90 days.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the data on your phone. Phone backups exclude ClearSkin data by design.
  • You can also email us to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to how it is processed. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, US state privacy and health-data laws, Tanzania’s Personal Data Protection Act 2022 or Kenya’s Data Protection Act 2019), you may also complain to your data protection regulator.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, stored in the app’s private storage on your phone, and can be protected with an optional biometric lock. Every paid feature is checked on our servers, purchases and rewards are verified with Google’s signatures, and no AI provider keys are ever in the app.

Not medical advice

ClearSkin gives cosmetic information. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
