Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

ClearSkin AI (“ClearSkin”, “we”) makes the ClearSkin AI mobile app. This policy explains what we process, why, and the choices you have.

In short

  • Your face photos, scan results, check-ins, products and coach chats are stored on your phone.
  • To analyse a scan or answer a coach question, the photo and the relevant skin details are sent, encrypted, to our AI provider. They are processed to give you a result and not stored on our servers.
  • Your account (your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google) is kept by our backend provider so you can sign in and keep Premium.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your photos to train AI.
  • You can export or permanently delete your data in the app at any time.

Before any photo leaves your phone, we remove its location and device information (EXIF). We do not collect your contacts, precise location or advertising ID.

Google account data

If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture link with ClearSkin. We use them only to create and secure your ClearSkin account. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers below that run the app. Deleting your account deletes it.

ClearSkin’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Service providers

  • Anthropic: AI analysis of scan photos and coach questions.
  • Supabase (Ireland): accounts and usage counters.
  • Google: Google sign-in, and delivery of sign-in code emails.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat: payments and subscription status.
  • Open-Meteo: weather for the city you choose.
  • Open Beauty Facts: product lookups (the barcode only).
  • PostHog (EU): anonymous analytics and crash reports.

Sensitive information

Skin photos and health-related details, such as pregnancy status or skin conditions, can count as sensitive information in some countries. We process them only with your explicit consent, given in the app before your first scan, and only to provide the features you use. We do not create face-recognition templates or identify you from photos.

Children

ClearSkin is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your choices and rights

  • Settings → Export my data gives you a copy of everything the app holds.
  • Settings → Delete account & all data deletes the data on your phone and your account and records on our servers.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the data on your phone. Phone backups exclude ClearSkin data by design.
  • You can also email us to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to how it is processed. Depending on where you live, you may also complain to your data protection regulator.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, stored in the app’s private storage on your phone, and can be protected with an optional biometric lock. Server access is limited to our backend functions.

Not medical advice

ClearSkin gives cosmetic information. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
